- **Wants to go deeper on:** the mathematical and architectural details of new methods: unfamiliar formulations, novel training objectives, world models, representation learning and theory.
- **Wants from a paper:**
  - what problem it solves and why the approach works
  - what is technically new and how the components interact
  - which assumptions it makes and what the experiments actually demonstrate
  - practical implications: compute and data requirements, scalability, training and inference cost, latency, parallelism, and how to implement or adapt the method in real systems
- **Depth:** technical and research-oriented. Intuition and the big picture first, then the architecture, algorithms, equations, training procedure and experiments. Don't oversimplify. Explain important equations and notation; skip long proofs unless they are essential to the contribution.
- **Style:** direct, precise and intellectually honest, with no hype. Keep what the paper demonstrates, what the authors claim and what can reasonably be inferred clearly separate. Use analogies only when they genuinely clarify, ideally from machine learning, robotics or software engineering.
- **Reading priorities:** the core contribution, technical mechanism, key design choices, experimental evidence, limitations and practical implications. Connect new concepts to related methods and say what is actually different from previous approaches.
- **Language:** English
